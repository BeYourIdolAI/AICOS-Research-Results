# AICOS — Research, Measurement and Assurance

This repository preserves research artifacts and their evidentiary boundaries.

`OBSERVATION ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ CLAIM ≠ DECISION ≠ PRODUCT VALUE`

Evidence outranks model confidence or consensus. Negative results and failed executions are intentionally retained. The artifact pipeline is `RAW → LOGS → RESULTS → REPORT`.

Current material includes `PROSPECTIVE-DIAG-2ARM-01`, with multiple harness iterations, preserved execution history, `EXECUTION_005` recorded as `INVALID_CONTROL`, and trace-integrity/pairwise analyses. It also includes separately frozen `PROSPECTIVE-MAGNITUDE-01`, whose completed `EXECUTION_001` reports a +26.67 percentage-point paired descriptive effect under its exact fixture.

These artifacts do not establish general model behavior, product readiness, security, or assurance validity.
