## Purpose

Record possible ways to obtain evidence capable of discriminating the
currently unresolved D9 residual-carrier alternatives for
`HIST-DIAG-CTRL-01`.

This plan does not select, authorize or execute an acquisition method.

## Evidence questions

Possible new evidence would need to address one or more of these unresolved
questions:

1. What exact quantitative relation was intended by `mätbart bättre` before
   the historical results were assessed?
2. Did additional observation-level raw responses or score records exist for
   the completed runs?
3. Can the reported Del A judgments be replayed from an independently
   established, complete source set without reconstructing missing runs?
4. Does Frida's complete contemporaneous classification-basis response, if it
   exists as an original record, address whether D9-level semantic and replay
   completeness was necessary to her bounded diagnosticity judgment?

## Possible acquisition paths

The following are unselected possibilities:

- locate a contemporaneous, pre-result definition or protocol amendment that
  gives `mätbart bättre` an exact quantitative meaning;
- locate original observation-level responses and mechanical score records
  whose provenance and historical dates can be established;
- obtain an evidence inventory from the historical test custodian identifying
  which raw and scoring artifacts existed, which were retained and which were
  never created;
- obtain Frida's complete original follow-up response with provenance showing
  that it was produced while she remained D1–D11-blind;
- construct a future, separately preregistered reproduction with explicit
  thresholds and complete observation-level preservation;
- commission an independent replay of Del A only if a complete, provenance-
  verified historical source set is first located.

## Minimum provenance controls for any future acquisition

Any later selected method would need to record:

- original source identity and custodian;
- acquisition date;
- whether the material existed before or after the historical result;
- exact bytes and SHA-256 for files;
- whether evidence is contemporaneous, retrospective or newly generated;
- whether the evidence is independent of D1–D11 exposure;
- all missing or unavailable items without reconstruction.
