# Frida pre-transfer in-scope test-design brief v0.1

STATUS: `FROZEN BEFORE FULL RESEARCH HANDOFF`

PURPOSE: `NEW EXPERIMENT DESIGN REQUEST`

## Request

Design a genuine two-arm control/attack experiment with:

- a fixed target proposition;
- a viable control;
- one intentionally varied attack property;
- at least two genuinely possible outcomes;
- criteria fixed before observation; and
- enough preserved provenance to replay the complete chain from observation to
  scoring to conclusion.

The experiment must be capable of producing both:

- a result supporting the hypothesis; and
- a result contradicting the hypothesis.

## Deliverables

Provide the proposed experiment as a self-contained design with:

- target proposition;
- control arm;
- attack arm;
- single varied attack property;
- all held-constant dimensions;
- possible observations and outcomes;
- preregistered scoring and conclusion rules;
- execution and raw-data format;
- provenance and replay requirements;
- explicit missing-data and invalid-output handling.

Do not execute the experiment. Freeze the design before any observation is
collected.

## Review boundary

Do not assess the design against any named existing gate set or predict whether
it would pass an external method. The design will be mechanically audited only
after it is frozen.
